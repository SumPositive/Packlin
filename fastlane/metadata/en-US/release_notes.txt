■ New "Tags" sorting style
You can now choose between the classic "Groups" and the new "Tags" for each pack.
- Groups: sort items strictly in layers of Pack, Group, and Item
- Tags: keep all items in one flat list and sort them loosely with tags
Attach several tags to an item and filter by tag. New packs start with Tags.

■ Bulk edit
Select items and add or remove tags, copy them to another pack, or delete them, all at once.

■ Share public packs with a link
Paste a share link (URL) into an email or social post and send it.
When the recipient taps the link, the public pack is imported into Packlin.
Packs using Tags can now be published too.

■ Easier input
- The keypad now has a built-in calculator
- Enter weights and counts with either a dial or a stepper
- Tapping outside the keyboard now closes it throughout the app

■ Easier to read
- In Item browse, switch the sort order between ascending and descending
- See the total weight of the items shown
- Per-item weights can now be shown in kg from 1,000 g
- Reworked the Settings screen, and the "Auto" text size that follows your device setting is now recommended
- Adjusted the color of the app icon

■ 6 new languages
Added German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.

■ Other
- Supports iOS 27
- Chappy: AI tickets are no longer sold. Tickets you already have can still be used. Without tickets, you can send a request by watching a video ad. We plan to offer a new AI service in the future.
- Minor display tweaks and stability improvements
