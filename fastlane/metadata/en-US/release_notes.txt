■ New "Tags" sorting style
You can now choose between the classic "Groups" and the new "Tags" for each pack.
- Groups: sort items strictly in layers of Pack, Group, and Item
- Tags: keep all items in one flat list and sort them loosely with tags

■ Share public packs with a link
Paste a share link (URL) into an email or social post and send it.
When the recipient taps the link, the public pack is imported into Packlin.

■ Easier input
- The keypad now has a built-in calculator
- Enter weights and counts with either a dial or a stepper
- Tapping outside the keyboard now closes it throughout the app

■ 6 new languages
Added German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.

■ Other
- Supports iOS 27
- Chappy: AI tickets are no longer sold. We're considering a new AI service.
