- Added Tags. Attach several tags to an item and filter by tag. Choose Tags or Groups for each pack; new packs start with Tags.
- In Item browse, you can now switch between ascending and descending order, and see the total weight of the items shown.
- Added Bulk edit. Select items and add or remove tags, copy them to another pack, or delete them, all at once.
- Choose between a stepper and a dial to adjust counts, and the keypad now has a built-in calculator.
- Per-item weights can now be shown in kg from 1,000 g.
- Added share links for public packs, and packs using Tags can now be published.
- Added German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.
- Reworked the Settings screen, and the "Auto" text size that follows your device setting is now recommended.
- Adjusted the color of the app icon.
- Minor display tweaks and stability improvements.
